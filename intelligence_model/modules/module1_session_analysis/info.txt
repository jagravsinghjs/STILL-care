# Module 1 — Session Analysis

Consumes a finished session's raw `turns` and turns them into a stored,
readable `SessionSummary`: per-turn distress scores, mean/max/min,
volatility, within-session trend, and a flagged-high-pause list.
`report_generator.py` renders that summary as a clinician-facing PDF.
Nothing predictive happens here — no trend forecasting, no risk scoring.
That's Modules 2 and 1's job; this module only describes what already
happened in the session.
