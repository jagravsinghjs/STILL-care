# Module 15 — Status & Risk Classification

Turns a continuous `EscalationRisk` into the three-color `RiskTier`
(green/yellow/red) supervisors actually look at, with hysteresis so the
dashboard doesn't flicker on noisy single-session data.

## Rules (locked-in)

- **Escalation** (GREEN→YELLOW, YELLOW→RED): 3 consecutive readings worse
  than the current tier, one tier at a time.
- **`acute_override`** bypasses all of this — forces RED immediately,
  regardless of streak state.
- **De-escalation, YELLOW→GREEN**: same flat 3-in-a-row rule as
  escalation.
- **De-escalation, RED→YELLOW**: weighted and more conservative. A `LOW`
  (GREEN-equivalent) reading counts double a `MODERATE` (YELLOW-equivalent)
  reading; the weighted total must reach 10 to step down (5 consecutive
  LOW readings, or 10 consecutive MODERATE, or any consecutive mix
  between). Any `HIGH` reading resets this to zero — "consecutive" is a
  hard requirement, not just a rolling average.

## State repurposing — read before touching this module

`risk_status.consecutive_high_assessments` is the *only* counter field
the schema provides. `hysteresis.py` repurposes it as a **signed streak
counter** — positive while escalating, negative while de-escalating,
reset to zero on any neutral reading. The column name no longer literally
describes its contents. This was a deliberate choice to avoid a schema
change; if a schema migration ever becomes acceptable, splitting this
into an explicit `streak_direction` + `streak_count` (or two separate
counters) would be more legible than the signed-integer trick used here.
